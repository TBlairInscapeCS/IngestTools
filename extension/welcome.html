How a job flows

Request
Open the request email in Outlook on the web; the panel grabs it, detects the client, and pulls the codes it asks for.
↓
Tracker
The tracking sheet loads from the email's link, an open sheet tab, or a manual pick. Matched rows show their line numbers - click one to highlight it in the sheet.
↓
Match
Email codes ↔ sheet rows. Exclude any row that doesn't belong; nothing downstream sees it.
↓
Download
Find & download tries direct links, then a delivery search, then any open page that lists files.
↓
Portal
Feeds files into portal.cognet.tv's ingest queue and fills Reporting ID / Title / Advertiser / Fingerprint. Standard batch first; _ticker versions build automatically and feed as a separate video-only batch.
↓
Report
Verify the records, review the drafted ticket, post to Jira when you're happy, copy the reply back to the requester.

First run

Open a request email in Outlook on the web, open this panel from the toolbar icon, and press ▶ Run all. The panel walks the rest; every stage shows a status dot, and anything destructive (BEGIN INGEST, Post to Jira) is always your click.

Optional connections sit collapsed above - the panel also names the one it needs, in place, when you use a feature that's missing it.
